02 · Race History

Where it started.

Some friendships begin in ordinary places. Ours began somewhere slightly more chaotic: a WhatsApp roleplay community.

Chapter 01 · The Beginning

Matías & Rafael

We met through a WhatsApp roleplay community inspired by La sociedad de la nieve. She played Matías Recalt, I played Rafael Federman. At the time, they were simply two characters in a roleplay. We had no idea that behind them, two people were about to become incredibly important to each other.

Chapter 02 · Beyond Roleplay

When the characters became friends

Little by little, we started talking more outside of roleplay. What began as conversations between two characters became a real friendship. Somewhere along the way, we simply clicked. And from there, everything became much more than just a roleplay.

Chapter 03 · Midnight Calls

Ten hours was never enough

There were countless nights playing together, talking until ridiculously late and falling asleep on calls. Sometimes we stayed connected for more than ten hours, until our phones were practically burning the next morning. Somehow, those became some of the memories I treasure the most.

Chapter 04 · Mamá & Hijo

Some things just stay

Somewhere along the way came one of our favourite little traditions: me calling you "mamá" and you calling me "hijo". It was silly, it was ours, and somehow it became one of those tiny pieces of our friendship that I will always remember.

Chapter 05 · The Drawing

A little piece of us

I still keep the drawing you made of us. I remember how happy it made me feel, and how I ended up crying because I was genuinely moved. It was "just" a drawing, but somehow it became something much bigger: a little piece of our friendship that means more to me than I could ever properly explain.

Chapter 06 · Mallorca

Finally, in the same place

In December 2025, I finally travelled to Mallorca to meet you. After so many messages, games, calls and nights together from behind a screen, we were finally in the same place. It was the moment when a friendship that had lived through screens suddenly became something I could experience in person.

Chapter 07 · Still Here

Two years later

We have been many things to each other: roleplay partners, friends, a couple, and now best friends. A lot has changed, but your way of caring, loving and worrying about the people you love has never stopped making you someone I choose to have in my life every single day.

Chapter 08 · The Road Ahead

There is still so much to come

This is not the end of our story. There are still more trips, more sleepless nights, more games, more laughs and memories waiting for us. I hope you get to be happy, chase every dream you have and see each one of them come true. And I hope that, wherever life takes us, there will always be another chapter waiting for us.
